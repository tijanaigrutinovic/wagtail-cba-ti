1. Introduction

Center for Business Acceleration (“CBA,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) uses cookies and similar storage technologies on our public website to provide core functionality, remember your preferences, and—with your consent—understand how visitors use our site.

This Cookie Policy should be read together with our Privacy Policy, which explains how we collect, use, and protect personal information more broadly.

Your choice: When you first visit our site, a cookie consent banner lets you accept or decline non-essential cookies. Essential cookies required for security and basic site operation are always used.

2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. Similar technologies include local storage (used by your browser to save data locally) and session storage.

Types by duration

  • Session cookies: Deleted when you close your browser.
  • Persistent cookies: Remain on your device for a set period or until you delete them.

Types by purpose

  • Strictly necessary: Required for the site to work (security, load balancing, consent storage).
  • Functional / preference: Remember choices such as display theme.
  • Analytics: Help us understand traffic and usage patterns (only loaded if you accept cookies).
  • Marketing: Used to deliver relevant advertising (we do not currently use marketing cookies on this site).

4. Cookie Consent Banner

On your first visit, we show a banner at the bottom of the page asking you to accept or decline non-essential cookies (such as analytics).

  • Accept All: We may load optional analytics scripts and set related cookies.
  • Decline: We do not load optional analytics scripts. Strictly necessary and preference cookies still apply so the site functions correctly.

Your choice is stored in your browser under the key cookie_consent with a value of accepted or declined. The banner will not appear again on the same browser unless you clear site data.

5. Managing Your Preferences

You can change or withdraw consent at any time by:

  • Clearing cookies and local storage for cbaeducation.com in your browser settings, then revisiting the site to see the consent banner again.
  • Using your browser’s privacy or cookie settings to block third-party or all cookies (note: blocking essential cookies may affect site functionality).
